DLAY 1.1.1 - installing
=======================

DLAY is an effect plug-in (VST3 on every system, Audio Unit on the Mac) and a standalone
app. This archive holds the installer for one system; use the section for yours.


WINDOWS
-------
1. Close your DAW.
2. Double-click the file that ends in   -Windows-Setup.exe
3. Windows may show a blue window, "Windows protected your PC". It appears for every
   program whose maker has not bought a signing certificate. Click   More info   and then
   Run anyway.
4. Click through the installer: Next, Install, Finish.
5. Open your DAW and let it look for new plug-ins.

The installer puts the plug-in in   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\   (the folder every
DAW looks in) and the standalone app in the Start menu.
To remove DLAY: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > DLAY > Uninstall.


MAC
---
1. Close your DAW.
2. Double-click the file that ends in   -macOS.pkg
3. macOS says it could not verify the package: it is not signed by a registered Apple
   developer. Close that message, open   System Settings > Privacy & Security,   scroll
   down to the line about DLAY and click   Open Anyway.
   (On macOS 14 and earlier you can instead right-click the package and choose Open.)
4. Click through the installer. It asks for the password of your Mac.
5. Open your DAW and let it look for new plug-ins.

The installer puts DLAY here, for every user of the Mac:
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/DLAY.vst3
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/DLAY.component     (Audio Unit: Logic, GarageBand)
    /Applications/DLAY.app
To remove DLAY, delete those three.


LINUX
-----
Copy   VST3/DLAY.vst3   to   ~/.vst3/   and rescan. The standalone is   Standalone/DLAY


UPDATING FROM AN EARLIER VERSION
--------------------------------
Close your DAW and run the installer of the new version. It replaces the old one, also a
copy that was put in place by hand. Projects and presets made with the earlier version
open as before. The version number is shown at the top right of the plug-in, next to the
ADVANCED key.


FL STUDIO
---------
1. OPTIONS > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
2. DLAY then appears among the effects. Click the star next to it to make it a favourite.
3. Open the Mixer, pick an insert, click an empty slot and choose DLAY.


FIRST STEPS
-----------
- Click the preset name at the top for the library (239 presets, searchable), or step
  through them with the arrows next to it.
- MIX sets how much echo you hear. FEEDBACK sets how long the echoes go on.
- TIME is a note value while SYNC is on, and milliseconds when it is off.
- On a send track set MIX to 100 % and switch on MENU > "Keep MIX when changing presets".
- Hover over any control for a description. Double-click a dial to reset it.
- ADVANCED (top right) opens the lower panel with three pages: TIME, SPACE and MOTION.
- Too small or too large? Drag the bottom-right corner of the window, or choose
  MENU > Window size. DLAY remembers the size with your project.
